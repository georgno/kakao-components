Squashy switches

Two vanilla Web Components with squash & stretch motion. Drag a thumb, tap a step, or use the keyboard.

Vanilla Shadow DOM web component — fully self-contained, single file.

Switch group

<squashy-switch-group> — on/off switches. Drag the thumb past an end and it squashes into the wall.

Step switch

<squashy-step-switch> — one thumb, several positions. It snaps to the nearest step; arrow keys move one step, Home and End jump to the ends.
