Range slider

<kakao-range-slider> - two thumbs on one track pick a span from–to. Same track, ticks and number fields as the slider; the thumbs never cross and can keep a minimum gap. Form-associated.

Variants

Configured through min, max, step, value="low,high", min-gap, unit and label. A press on the track moves the nearer thumb.

Price, step 10

Minimum gap · min-gap="5"

The thumbs stop 5 apart; Home and End go to each thumb's own limit.

Fine steps · native number fields

Thumbs may meet

With both thumbs on one spot, the first drag direction picks the thumb.

Touch steppers · touch-steppers

Shown automatically on touch screens: 44px buttons either side of each field, hold to repeat.

Disabled

Themed

Every setting falls back to the slider's (--kakao-slider-accent …), so both stay in step unless one is set apart.

In a form

One name, two entries: low first, then high.

Submit to see the FormData entries.
