Flow

<kakao-flow> - a vertical flow: one even gap between its children, none before the first or after the last, whatever margins the page gives headings and paragraphs. For the sections of a page, the fields of a form, the blocks of a card. It is light DOM: flow.css spaces it, so a server-rendered page needs no script. For columns there is the grid; stack is a pile of overlapping cards, not a layout.

Even gaps

The heading, the paragraph and the card each bring their own margins; inside the flow only the gap counts (dashed: the flow's edges).

Gap sizes · nested

gap is sm (0.5rem: a heading and its text), md (1rem: blocks) or lg (2rem: sections). A flow inside a flow sits at its parent's gap and spaces its own children by its own.
