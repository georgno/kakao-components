Toast

<kakao-toast> - short messages floating in a corner of the viewport, stacked like cards: the newest in front, the older ones behind it. Point at the stack or tab into it and the cards fan out. Every toast is a <kakao-alert>, so tones, icons, colours and the close button are the alert's.

Tones · KakaoToast.show()

Info and success leave after 8 seconds (--kakao-alert-duration); warning and danger stay until closed. Showing a toast never moves focus; the region is a polite live region and each toast keeps the alert's role.

Stack · max

At most max toasts are visible (default 3); older ones wait hidden behind the stack, with their timers stopped, and come forward as newer ones leave. While the stack is fanned out (pointer or focus), every timer in it pauses. On touch, tap the stack to fan it out.

Corner · position

bottom-right (default), bottom-left, top-right, top-left. The newest toast is nearest the corner. Top corners sit below the header bar; below 30rem the stack spans the screen. An aside menu opening on the same side pushes the stack beside its panel when there is room.

Timer, action and handle

duration in ms sets the time for any tone; 0 keeps the toast. action adds a button under the message that closes the toast unless its onClick returns false. show() returns a handle with close() and a closed promise.

A message that disappears can be gone before someone using a screen reader or magnifier, or reading slowly, gets to it. Keep toasts short, and don't put the only copy of important information in one.

Written toasts

A <kakao-alert> written into <kakao-toast> (server flash messages) or appended to it later becomes a toast; its own duration is the toast's time. Toasts already in the page at load are shown but not announced.

Attributes: position, max, label, expanded (set by the region). Method: show(options); helper KakaoToast.show(options) with tone, heading, message, duration, dismissible, closeLabel, action, region. Events: toast-show, toast-hidden (with reason). Full reference in the header of toast.js.
