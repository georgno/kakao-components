Date input

<kakao-date-input> - The browser's own date, time, date-and-time, month and week field and picker, dressed as the library's other fields. The value is always ISO. Form-associated.

Min · max

Today shortcut · today

A tag in the row under the field sets the current day, time, month or week in one click, as a user edit (input and change fire once each). Its label follows the locale; today-label overrides it. Outside min / max it stays, disabled, and says why: in its title on hover and in its accessible description for keyboard and screen reader users.

Beside the other fields

Locale

The field passes the locale (the locale attribute, else the nearest lang) to the native input. The browser still draws the field in its own format: Chromium in the browser's language, Firefox in its language or the system's regional format, Safari in the system's region. None of them reads lang. What a page can format itself is shown below each field.
