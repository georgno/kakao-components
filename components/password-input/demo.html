Password input

<kakao-password-input> - A password field with a show / hide button and a Caps Lock warning. Opt-in requirements are listed under the field, and their progress is drawn with <kakao-progress>: one segment per requirement. Form-associated.

Sign in

Basic · show / hide

The eye button shows the password as text and back; the caret stays where it was. Turn Caps Lock on while typing to see the warning.

Requirements · requirements

The page loads progress.js too: the bar is a <kakao-progress> with one segment per requirement, so every requirement the password meets completes a segment, and that segment pops. The bar turns to the success colour once all are met. Requirements are advice only, unless required-strength is set.

Blocking the form · required-strength

With required-strength unmet requirements make the field invalid, so the form doesn't submit. Empty means all of them; a number means at least that many. It turns requirements on, so the person always sees what's asked.
