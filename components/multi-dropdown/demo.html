Multi dropdown

<kakao-multi-dropdown> — separate dropdowns joined in an input group that hold one value, e.g. a date dd.mm.yyyy as Day · Month · Year. Segments can be “Unknown”, so 00.00.1990 means “sometime in 1990”.

Dates

type="date" builds day, month and year. Impossible days are disabled; a day that no longer fits the month moves to its last day and comes back when it fits again.
