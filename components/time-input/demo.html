Time input

<kakao-time-input> - The browser's own time field and picker, dressed as the library's other fields. It shows 12 or 24 hours as the browser and the system are set; the value is always ISO 24-hour. The sibling of date input. Form-associated.

A tag in the row under the field sets the current time in one click, rounded to step (without one, the minute), as a user edit (input and change fire once each). Its label follows the locale; now-label overrides it. Outside min / max it stays, disabled, and says why: in its title on hover and in its accessible description for keyboard and screen reader users.

Beside the other fields

12 or 24 hours

The field passes the locale (the locale attribute, else the nearest lang) to the native input. The browser still draws the field in its own format, 12-hour with AM/PM or 24-hour: Chromium as the browser's language, Firefox as its language or the system's regional format, Safari as the system's region. None of them reads lang. What a page can format itself is shown below the field.
