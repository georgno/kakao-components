Alert

<kakao-alert> - an inline message in the page content: an icon, an optional title and the message, in one of four tones. It can be closed, and info and success alerts fade out by themselves after 8 seconds.

Tones · tone

Info (default), success, warning and danger (error works too). Warning and danger get role="alert", info and success role="status". These examples have duration="0" so they stay.

Fading out · duration

Info and success alerts hide after --kakao-alert-duration (8 seconds), warning and danger stay. duration sets the time in ms for any tone; duration="0" turns it off. The timer pauses while the pointer is over the alert, while focus is inside it, and while the tab is hidden. With reduced motion there is no fade; the alert still goes.

A message that disappears can be gone before someone using a screen reader or magnifier, or reading slowly, gets to it. Don't put the only copy of important information in an alert that hides itself.
