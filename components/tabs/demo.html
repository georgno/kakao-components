Playground

Programmatic selection and adding/removing tabs at runtime.

API: select(controlsOrUrlOrItem), event tab-change {controls, item, url}. <kakao-tab> attributes: controls / data-controls, href / data-url, selected, disabled, data-no-select (host for an embedded control). Full reference in the header of tabs.js.
