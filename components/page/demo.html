Page

<kakao-page> - the page container: the content column at a readable maximum width, centred, with side padding (and room for a phone's notch). It starts just below the sticky header bar and keeps its end clear of a fixed action bar. It is light DOM: page.css lays it out, so a server-rendered page needs no script. Inside it, the flow spaces the sections and the grid lays out cards.

The page's content column

Put it in the <main> below the header bar; it is not a landmark itself. Dashed: the container's edges, its side padding inside them.

Narrow: one column of text or a form

Widths

width is the maximum width, padding included: narrow (45rem), normal (72rem, the default), wide (96rem: tables and dashboards) or full (no limit). Below its width the container spans the screen.
