Card

<kakao-card> — a content card with header, body and footer. When the footer starts with a <kakao-button>, a faint glow in the button's colour rises from behind it.

Theme trigger glow

A card without a footer button can still glow when the <kakao-theme-switch> sends its colour through the KakaoComponents trigger API. Switch the page theme to see the glow follow --kakao-color-primary.
