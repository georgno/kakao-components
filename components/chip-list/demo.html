Chip list

<kakao-chip-list> - a wrapping row of chips. When one leaves, it fades out where it was and the others glide to their new places, across row breaks too. The list's height follows instead of jumping.

Removing

Remove chips with the × (mouse, or Tab and Enter). Chips at the start of a row slide up to the end of the row above.
