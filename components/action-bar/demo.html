Action bar

<kakao-action-bar> - a floating bar for actions on the items someone has selected: a short summary and a row of buttons. It sticks to the bottom (or top) of its container, or is fixed to the viewport. With count it slides in while something is selected and away at 0, and screen readers hear the new count. For a passing notice there is the toast.

Sticky in its container · count

Tick a few rows. The bar is the last child of the scrolling list, so it floats at the list's bottom edge while you scroll and rests at its end. The page sets count and writes the summary; a count of 0 hides the bar.

At the top · position="top"

The bar goes at the start of its container and floats at the top edge; on a page it stays below the header bar. Smaller buttons suit a dense list: give the bar the same size="sm", so the space around them stays even and the corners concentric.

Fixed to the viewport · fixed

Centred at the bottom of the screen, clear of the safe-area insets. On a phone it spans the width, flush with the screen edge. While it is shown it writes its height to --kakao-action-bar-rendered-height, so the page can pad its end (this page does).

Announcements · count-label

A polite live region reads the new count a moment after it changes: the summary's text by default, count-label (with {count}) when set, else “3 selected”. The fixed bar above announces “Selected rows: 4”. The count at load and a drop to 0 are not announced.

Hidden · hidden

Without count the bar is shown; hidden hides it at once, and removing it slides the bar back in. A hidden bar takes no space.
