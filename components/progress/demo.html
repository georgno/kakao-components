Progress

<kakao-progress> - a progress bar split into segments. The value fills them in order, and every segment the fill completes gives a short pop. Without a value it is indeterminate.

Step through it

Step the value and watch each segment pop as the fill completes it. Jump past several and they pop one after the other. Going down and back up inside a segment doesn't pop again; a segment pops again only after it has emptied.

Segments · segments

Default 4. segments="1" is one plain bar. The segment the value lands in fills partly.

Indeterminate

No value: a highlight sweeps through the segments. With reduced motion it fades in place instead.

Sizes · size and tones · tone

No visible label

Without label, name the bar with aria-label.
Attributes: value, max (default 1), segments (default 4), label, show-value, value-text, size (sm · md · lg), tone (primary · success · warning · danger, alias error). Properties: value, max, segments, position. Event: progress-segment-complete. Full reference in the header of progress.js.
