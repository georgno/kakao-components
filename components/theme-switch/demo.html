Theme switch

<kakao-theme-switch> — switches the page between colour themes: light, beige, umber, dark, AMOLED, Catppuccin and Forest. The button spreads the new theme as a ripple; the two theme-picker controls cross-fade instead. The choice is remembered; until then the page follows the operating system. Any control can pick a theme, and the two theme-picker forms follow the pointer: a <kakao-button> menu and a <kakao-step-switch>.

Variants

All switches on a page stay in sync, also across tabs.

Dropdown · kakao-button menu

Picks any theme, also ones the switch doesn't cycle through. Run the pointer down the list and the page cross-fades after it; the check mark and the label move with the theme. No page script: theme-switch.js wires it.

Step switch · theme-picker

The page follows the thumb: drag it across the track and every step it crosses changes the theme, also the ones it only flies over when you tap further away. The page cross-fades between themes instead of rippling, so nothing freezes under the pointer. No page script: theme-switch.js wires it.

Themed components

Accessibility

Every theme has to pass node tools/check-themes.js: text 4.5:1, borders, focus and filled controls 3:1.

With reduce motion turned on in the system, the theme changes at once, without the ripple or the icon morph. Keyboard activation starts the ripple at the button. A polite live region announces the new theme. With the system's increase contrast setting, muted text and borders get stronger in every theme.

Playground

Drive the first switch by hand.

Attributes: list-data, storage-key, show-label, disabled. Events: theme-change. Pickers follow the pointer via step-cross (step switch) and kakao-button-menu-hover (menu), and pick via kakao-button-select. Themes live in shared/themes.css. Full reference in the header of theme-switch.js.
