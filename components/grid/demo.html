Grid

<kakao-grid> - a responsive grid: as many equal columns as fit at a minimum width, one column on a phone, with a gap from the spacing tokens. For cards, stat tiles and fields side by side. It is light DOM: grid.css lays it out, so a server-rendered page needs no script. For rows of data there is the table or the grid view; for one column of sections, the flow.

Cards that wrap

The default minimum is 18rem: two to four columns on a laptop, one on a phone. Every cell of a row is as tall as the tallest.

Minimum width · gap

min="sm" (12rem) fits more tiles in a row, min="lg" (24rem) fewer; gap is sm (0.5rem), md (1rem) or lg (2rem). Any other minimum: style="--kakao-grid-min: 14rem".
