Stepper

<kakao-stepper> - where someone is in a multi-step flow: numbered steps that are done, current or upcoming. It shows progress; the page owns the step content.

Steps and the current one

Steps come from list-data, the current one from current (a 0-based index). Steps before it get a check mark, steps after it stay outlined. A screen reader hears an ordered list with "Done" on finished steps and the current step marked.

Labels only

With descriptions, current by value

current also takes the value of a step that has one.

Start, middle and finished

A current equal to the number of steps means the flow is finished: every step is done.

Step states

"state": "error" marks a step as failed: danger colour, an exclamation mark, and the word "Error" for screen readers. "state": "done" marks a step as done wherever it sits, for a step that was skipped or prefilled.

Vertical

orientation="vertical" puts the markers in a column with the labels beside them. It suits a side column and longer descriptions.

Narrow spaces

A horizontal stepper needs room for its labels. When a step would get less than --kakao-stepper-min-step-width (6rem), it collapses to one line, "Step 2 of 4" with the current label, over a bar with a segment per step. It goes by the stepper's own width, not the window's, so it also collapses in a narrow column. On a phone this keeps the form in view; a vertical list would push it down. Screen readers still get the whole list.

Clickable steps

By default nothing in a stepper is focusable. With clickable the steps before the current one become buttons; with clickable="all" every step but the current one. A button fires a cancelable step-select; unless it is cancelled the stepper moves there and fires stepper-change. Tab to a step, Enter or Space to pick it; the arrow keys, Home and End move between the buttons.

A small wizard

next(), previous() and goTo(indexOrValue) move the stepper from script; next() on the last step finishes the flow. The page shows the content for stepper-change and moves focus to it.

Stepper or step switch?

The names are close, the jobs are not. A step switch is a form control: a thumb that picks one of several values, like a radio group. A stepper picks nothing: it is a list that says how far along a flow is.

Stepper · progress through a flow, an ordered list with a current step.

Step switch · a setting with a value, a radio group with a thumb to drag.

Other words

KakaoStepper.texts sets the words for every stepper: done and error (read by screen readers), summary and complete (the compact line).
