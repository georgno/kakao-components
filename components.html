Select

<kakao-select> Construction Open demo →

Pick one value, or several with multiple, from a list with a search field on top: typing filters as you go, ignoring case and accents. The field looks like the text input; the panel is the library's dropdown and stays on screen. Options come from child <option> / <optgroup> elements, list-data or the options property. Form-associated: several values become several form entries.

Combobox

<kakao-combobox> Construction Open demo →

A text field with suggestions: typing filters the list (ignoring case and accents), and you either pick a suggestion or keep your own text, offered as a “Use …” row at the top. autocomplete="both" completes the first match in the field; strict allows only listed values. The field is the text input; the list is the select's dropdown. Form-associated, with pattern, minlength and maxlength for free text.

Date input

<kakao-date-input> Construction Open demo →

The browser's own date, time, date-and-time, month and week field and picker, dressed as the library's other fields: label, hint, error, three sizes, the picker icon where the select's chevron sits.
