One tab stop with arrow keys, Home / End and type-ahead. selection is single (a filled row), multiple (checkboxes; a parent ticks everything under it and shows a dash when only some are ticked) or none; with name the selected ids are sent with a form. Lazy nodes load their children on tree-expand.
