Theme switch

<kakao-theme-switch> Construction Open demo →

Switches the page between colour themes with a ripple from the button. The switch toggles light and dark and reveals the new theme with a ripple; the menu and the step switch pick any of the seven themes (Light, Beige, Umber, Dark, AMOLED, Catppuccin, Forest) and both follow the pointer, cross-fading as the pointer runs down the menu or the thumb crosses a step. Remembers the choice; follows the system until then. Themes live in shared/themes.css and must pass tools/check-themes.js.
